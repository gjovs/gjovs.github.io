## whoami

I build backend systems that turn complex operations into reliable software. I currently work as a Backend Engineer at **Shopee**, designing the services, asynchronous workflows and integrations behind data- and ML-driven products, as well as their operational tools.

Node.js/TypeScript remains my primary stack, complemented by advanced Python work with FastAPI in backend and model-serving systems. I also work with Fastify, PostgreSQL, Redis and asynchronous workers. When the product needs it, I work across Next.js/React frontends. My work with AI is grounded in backend engineering: fine-tuning multimodal models such as SigLIP2, serving BGE-M3 embeddings and sentiment/image-matching models through GPU-backed FastAPI services, and building the queues, caching, persistence and observability around them.

Before Shopee, I built payment integrations and audit-friendly microservices in PCI-DSS-regulated environments. That background still shapes how I think about reliability, data integrity and the boring details that keep production running.
